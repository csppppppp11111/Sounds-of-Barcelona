ABOUT

SOUNDS OF BARCELONA is a collective archive of the sounds of the city: markets, metro, birds, voices, bells, construction work.

The sphere is Barcelona, with its real relief: the sea, Montjuïc and the city rising to the hills of Collserola. Every sound leaves a mark in the place where it was recorded. Low sounds make wide marks, loud sounds make tall marks, high sounds add small bumps around them. When a sound plays, its neighbourhood ripples.

Anyone can add a sound. The archive is then turned into an object: a sphere that keeps only the marks of the sounds, to 3D print.

SOUNDS OF BARCELONA

AN ARCHIVE OF THE CITY'S SOUNDS

ABOUT

SOUNDS OF BARCELONA is a collective archive of the city's sounds: markets, metro, birds, voices, bells, construction. Barcelona keeps changing, and so does the way it sounds. Anyone can add a recording made in Barcelona, with the street, the day and what we can hear.

HOW TO READ THE SPHERE

  • ◯ The top of the sphere is Barcelona, with its real relief: the sea in front, Montjuïc, the city rising to the Collserola hills.
  • ◉ Every sound leaves a round mark where it was recorded. Click a mark to hear it.
  • ⌒ Low sounds (metro, traffic) make wide marks.
  • ▲ Loud sounds make tall marks.
  • ∘ High sounds (birds, bells) add small bumps around the mark.
  • ≈ When a sound plays, its neighbourhood ripples with it.

THE OBJECT

GENERATE OBJECT exports a sphere with a flat base, carrying the marks of every sound in the archive, but not the city. A silent version of Barcelona, to 3D print. Each print is a portrait of the archive on one day.
